project

GT Movies Store

GT Movies Store is a Django web application that lets users browse detailed information about movies and place orders to purchase them. Beyond browsing, users can also list, create, edit, and delete their own movie reviews — giving the app a genuine community layer on top of the core storefront. Each movie listing includes its name, price, description, and an image, all served through a clean, user-friendly interface.

Django Python SQLite HTML/CSS
  • Movie catalog: Displays all available movies with their name, price, description, and image, giving users a clear overview of what's available to purchase.
  • Movie detail & purchase: Lets a user view full details on a single movie and place an order to purchase it.
  • Review management: Users can list, create, edit, and delete their own movie reviews, giving the app a real community layer beyond just browsing.
  • User-friendly interface: The overall layout and navigation are designed to make browsing, reviewing, and purchasing feel intuitive rather than cluttered.
process

How I built it

Approach

I broke the project up across multiple sessions rather than trying to do it in one push, which made it easier to come back with a clearer head and catch things I'd missed.

Navigating questions & doubts

Whenever I got stuck, I gave myself 20 minutes to really try to work through the problem on my own. If I still hadn't found a solution after that, I'd reach out to a peer for help or check Ed Discussion.

What I'd do differently

If I could go back, I'd have recorded my conversations with peers and the solutions I found through Ed Discussion as I worked through problems, rather than only afterward. At the time, I was worried about keeping the final video concise and didn't want to risk it running long, so I held off on recording. In hindsight, it would have been easy enough to simply trim the footage down during editing — capturing the process as it happened would have made for a much richer look at how the project actually came together.
